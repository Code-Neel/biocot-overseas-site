03BUSINESS SERVICES

Practical support
for cross-border business.

The company poster describes services focused on Nigeria and Tanzania. Scope, eligibility, documents, and outcomes depend on each client’s case and the relevant authorities.

01

Company registration support

Guidance and document coordination for registration processes in Nigeria and Tanzania.

Scope: Registration is completed by the relevant government authority; requirements and approval vary by jurisdiction.

02

Organic documentation & GOTS guidance

Documentation support for eligible businesses exploring organic textile supply-chain certification.

Scope: GOTS applies to eligible textile supply chains. Independent GOTS-approved certification bodies assess and certify; Biocot does not issue or guarantee certification. Read the official GOTS standard ↗

03

Import & export coordination

Support discussing product sourcing, buyer requirements, shipping documents, and cross-border trade steps.

Scope: Import permits, customs rules, and product requirements must be confirmed for each product and destination.

04

Visa application assistance

Administrative guidance with application steps and document preparation for business or travel cases.

Scope: Eligibility, decisions, and processing times are determined by the issuing authorities; no outcome is guaranteed.
