01ABOUT THE MATERIAL

A trade name that needs clear product identification.

“Black Stone Flower” is a spice-market name commonly associated with dried lichen material, rather than a conventional flower. Names and species can vary between markets and supply regions, so a trade name alone is not enough to confirm the botanical identity of a shipment.

For a serious buying discussion, request the supplier’s lot description and supporting identification. Any scientific name, grade, or origin statement should match the actual lot documents before it appears on a purchase order or public product specification.
